Philosophy

How I actually work

Curiosity-driven, product-minded, and allergic to complexity that exists for its own sake.

01

Simplicity as a goal

Always searching for the simplest, most direct path to meaningful impact. If it takes a diagram to explain to the team, the user will need one too.

02

Collaboration first

High-performance teams beat heroic individuals. I work cross-functionally so design stays honest to user needs and to where the business is actually going.

03

Continuous iteration

Data-backed insight and systematic prototyping, on repeat. I'd rather validate a rough prototype this week than defend a beautiful mock next quarter.
